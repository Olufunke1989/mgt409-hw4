Test 02

Dynamic Product Search Results

Passed

What I tested

On the Products page I opened the chat and asked "What hoodies do you have?" I wanted to see whether the chatbot would search my real catalogue and show the hoodies as product cards on the website. Then I clicked one of the cards.

What happened

The chatbot said we have 27 hoodies, from $45.00 to $88.00. A "Results for 'Hoodies'" section appeared right away with 12 cards (and a "See all 27" button). Each card had its photo, name, price and short description, and all 12 images loaded.

This kind of simple question is answered instantly straight from the database (the reply is marked "⚡ Instant answer from our catalogue").

Clicking Basic Hoodie Big Yale opened /products/basic-hoodie-big-yale.

Passed ✓

The cards came from my real catalogue, the count and price range match the database, and the card opened the correct product page.

Test 03

Product Search and Filtering Usability Test

Passed

What I tested

This is one of my Problem 9 usability improvements. On the Products page I typed "hoodie" into the search box, then picked Size L under "Size in stock" and $60 – $80 under "Price".

What happened

The results updated on their own after each step, with no need to press Search or reload the page:

102 products → 27 after typing "hoodie" → 20 after picking size L → 17 after picking $60 – $80.

Each filter showed up as a removable tag, and the count line says "17 products · all $68.00".

Result

Passed ✓

The numbers at every step match what my database says, and every product left is a hoodie in stock in L between $60 and $80.
